Every piece runs small. Every drop sells out. That's not a flaw in the plan — it's the plan.

We didn't set out
to fit in.

Built Different started as a rejection of the safe stuff — the plain tees, the logo-in-the-corner basics. We took the elegance of an old signature and stitched it onto gear built for movement: racing piping, jersey numbers, camo panels, plaid raglans.

Every drop is dated. Every piece is numbered. MMXXV and counting.
